About Vibely

Vibely is a social media platform designed to help people connect, share moments, discover new people and communicate.

Users can create accounts, add profile pictures, share text/photo/video posts, like and comment, follow users, message each other and receive notifications.

This version is a browser-based project, so account and media data are stored locally in the browser.
